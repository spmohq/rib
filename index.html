Input Clarity Guide

Self-Correction Protocol

  1. Replace the placeholders with inputs.
  2. Copy the entire prompt chain and paste into ChatGPT.
  3. Wait for response and read all answers.
  4. Apply targeted corrections (e.g., “Compress Phase 3 by 30%” or “Stop after Phase 5; no paid asset” or “Re-run Phase 1 with a more advanced audience”).

Use this guide to self-correct your inputs before running the prompt.

1️⃣ Primary Platform

Where the struggle is happening

Ask yourself:
“Where is my audience actively trying—and failing—to make progress right now?”
👍 Good examples
  • Facebook
  • LinkedIn
  • Email newsletter
👎 Bad examples
  • Social media
  • Online
  • Multiple platforms
🧠 Why this matters: Locating the problem in one real environment allows the system to reason with context.

2️⃣ Monetisation Model

How money would come in if this worked

Ask yourself:
“If this problem were solved, how would money eventually show up in their life?”
👍 Good examples
  • Facebook content monetisation
  • Selling digital products
  • Coaching / consulting
👎 Bad examples
  • Gumroad
  • Funnels
  • Launches / Ads
🧠 Why this matters: This describes the money mechanism, not just the tool or tactic used to get it.

3️⃣ Target Audience

Who feels this problem most sharply

Ask yourself:
“Who is frustrated by this problem often enough that they would actually act?”
👍 Good examples
  • Aspirant digital creators
  • Solo creators under 10k followers
  • Freelancers building an audience
👎 Bad examples
  • Creators
  • Beginners
  • Everyone
🧠 Why this matters: Specific groups have shared psychology. Broad groups lead to weak outputs.

4️⃣ Audience Situation MOST IMPORTANT

The exact moment they are stuck

Ask yourself:
“What are they trying to do—and where does their confidence break?”
👍 Good examples
  • Knows content matters but doesn’t know what to post next
  • Posting consistently but not earning money
👎 Bad examples
  • Wants to grow faster
  • Needs a content strategy
⚠️ Note: If this input is weak, the entire system weakens. Describe the lived "stuck" moment.

5️⃣ Primary Outcome

What “relief” looks like to them

Ask yourself:
“If this worked, what would make them say: ‘Okay, now I know what to do’?”
👍 Good examples
  • Know exactly what to post and why
  • Turn effort into predictable results
👎 Bad examples
  • Build a 7-figure business
  • Achieve freedom / Scale massively
🧠 ONE OVERRIDING RULE
"Describe reality, not ambition.
Confusion, not clarity.
Stuckness, not success."

Always choose the uncomfortable truth over the polished answer.

✅ FINAL SELF-CHECK (30s)

Before running, ask yourself:

  • Would a real person say, "Yes, that's me"?
  • Does this describe now, not "someday"?
  • Does this avoid sounding like marketing copy?
